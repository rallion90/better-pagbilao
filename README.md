# Better Pagbilao

**Live site: [betterpagbilao.org](https://betterpagbilao.org)**

Better Pagbilao is a community-built, volunteer-run civic portal for **Pagbilao, Quezon**. It gathers government services, hotlines, tourism information and public documents in one place, so residents can find what they need without hunting across offices and social media pages.

It is built in the same spirit as [BetterGov.ph](https://bettergov.ph).

> **Not an official government website.** Better Pagbilao is an independent, volunteer project. It is not run by or affiliated with the Municipality of Pagbilao or any government office. Always confirm requirements, fees and schedules with the responsible office before you act on anything you read here.

## What you can find

| Section | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Government** | Legislative council, local officials directory |
| **Services** | Health services, disaster and safety, agriculture and livelihood, social welfare, business and permits |
| **Announcements** | Public notices and updates |
| **Hotlines** | Emergency and office contact numbers |
| **Explore** | History, barangays, gateway location |
| **Transparency** | Ordinances and executive orders, procurement, Citizen's Charter, permits and clearances |

### Citizen assistant

The site includes a chat assistant that answers questions about Pagbilao in English, Tagalog or Taglish. It answers only from the verified information on the site. If the site has no verified answer, it says so and does not guess.

## Contributing

Accurate information matters more than anything else here. You can help by:

- **Reporting a wrong or outdated detail**, such as a phone number, schedule, requirement or fee. Open an issue and include a source, such as an official page, a notice or the office itself.
- **Suggesting missing information** that residents would look for.
- **Improving the site**, such as fixing bugs, accessibility, translations or performance. Please open an issue first for larger changes.

When you report a fact, please say where it comes from. Unsourced changes can't be merged.
